What we do

Whole Harted Wellness LLC is a small software company. We design, build and run our own products rather than handing them over at launch, so the businesses using them have someone who answers when something needs fixing.

Our work is aimed at operations that run on shift work, where getting the schedule right decides whether the day goes smoothly.

Our products

Schedule Maxxing

Staff scheduling for clubs, restaurants and hospitality teams. Coverage requirements by role and location, schedules generated automatically around them, time off, shift trades, department checklists and minor labor rules, all in one place.
